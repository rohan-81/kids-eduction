Children's Privacy Policy

Designed for Families & Kids (Ages 2–8) • COPPA & GDPR-K Compliant

Last Updated: November 2026

Core Commitment: KidsZone is an educational platform designed with children's safety and privacy as the foremost priority. We do not sell personal data, do not display third-party advertisements, and do not collect unnecessary personal information from children.

1. Information We Collect

A. From Children (Under Age 13):

  • Nicknames & Avatars: Children are invited to select a friendly nickname (e.g., "Rohan", "Little Star") and a cartoon companion avatar. We do not require or request real legal names, photos, or government IDs from children.
  • Learning Progress & Activity: We record educational milestones such as completed lessons, quiz stars, virtual coins, and streaks. This data is stored locally on the device (Guest Session) or under the parent's verified account (Cloud Sync).
  • Microphone Usage (Speech Practice): If pronunciation practice features are used, voice audio is processed locally on the device to detect speech activity. Voice recordings are never stored or uploaded to remote servers.

B. From Parents and Guardians:

  • Account Details: When a parent registers an account, we collect an email address and a securely hashed password to facilitate multi-device cloud backup and password recovery.
  • Parental Controls: Parents can set a 4-digit PIN, daily screen time limits, and eye-care rest reminders.

2. Third-Party Advertising & Tracking

  • No Commercial Ads: KidsZone contains zero third-party advertisements.
  • No Behavioral Tracking: We do not track children across third-party websites or services for targeted advertising.

3. Parental Rights & Controls (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Parents and legal guardians retain full ownership and control over their family's data:

  • Review & Inspection: Parents can view their child's detailed learning history, activity logs, and badges at any time via the Parent Control Dashboard.
  • Data Deletion: Parents may delete child profiles, reset learning progress, or delete their entire family account directly through the settings or by contacting our support team.
  • Parental Gate: External links, app purchases, and parental settings are strictly protected behind a parental gate (mathematical challenge + 4-digit security PIN) to prevent accidental toddler access.

4. Data Storage & Security

We implement rigorous technical safeguards including encrypted network communications (HTTPS/TLS), cryptographic password hashing (PBKDF2-SHA256), and restricted database permissions to protect stored information against unauthorized access.

5. Contact Information

For questions or requests regarding this Children's Privacy Policy or your family's data, please contact the application publisher through the official support channel designated on the application store listing or by writing to the registered developer support email.
